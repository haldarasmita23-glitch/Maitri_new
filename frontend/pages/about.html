About Maitri

Built for Peenya and Nagasandra — a community platform with a local heart.

Our Story

Why We Built Maitri

Maitri (Sanskrit: मैत्री — friendship, goodwill) was born from a simple observation:
the best local shops are often the hardest to find.

Peenya is one of India's largest industrial areas. Nagasandra is a fast-growing residential neighbourhood. Together, they are home to thousands of factory workers, students, and families — all of whom rely on the same small cluster of local businesses every single day.

Yet discovering a reliable dosa stall near Peenya Gate 2, a blouse tailor in Nagasandra, or a trustworthy phone repair shop used to mean asking a neighbour or hunting through WhatsApp groups. Maitri changes that.

Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Maitri free to use?

Yes — completely free. Residents can browse, search, and review vendors without paying anything. Vendors can list their business at no charge.

How do I list my business?

Click "List Your Business" anywhere on the site and fill in your details. Your listing will be reviewed by our team and approved within 24–48 hours.

Are the vendors verified?

We manually review each vendor submission and verify the phone number and location before approval. Verified vendors receive a ✅ badge on their listing.

Which areas does Maitri cover?

Currently Peenya (1st, 2nd Stage, Industrial Area) and Nagasandra. We plan to expand to Jalahalli, Yeshwanthpur, and surrounding areas in future phases.

Can I report a wrong listing or a bad experience?

Yes. A complaints and reporting system is being built and will be available in a later phase. For now, please write an honest review to inform other users.
